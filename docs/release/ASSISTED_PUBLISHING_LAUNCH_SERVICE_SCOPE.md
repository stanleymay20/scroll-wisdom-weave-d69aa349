# Assisted Publishing Launch — service scope

Status: Pre-sale contract draft. This package must remain unavailable until operational ownership, response targets and human staffing are confirmed.

Price: $399 one-time

## Included

- one defined ScrollLibrary publication project;
- eligibility for up to 3 format-specific ScrollLibrary Press ISBN assignments after validation;
- human metadata QA for title, subtitle, contributor names, imprint, description and publication metadata;
- one title/subtitle positioning review;
- one book-description/blurb review;
- category and keyword preparation checklist for supported distribution channels;
- validation of publication-format records for paperback, hardcover and EPUB where requested and eligible;
- barcode preparation for eligible print formats after ISBN assignment;
- pre-distribution production checklist covering trim/format metadata, cover/interior readiness and required rights declarations;
- distribution-preparation checklist for supported external channels; this is preparation assistance, not a guarantee that a third-party retailer will accept or list the title;
- one launch-readiness checklist covering metadata, files, pricing inputs and release sequencing;
- up to 2 consolidated human review/revision rounds on the publishing setup and metadata deliverables;
- email/support-channel assistance for the covered publishing-service order until the included deliverables are completed or the service window expires.

## Not included unless separately purchased or explicitly stated

- ghostwriting or substantive rewriting of the manuscript;
- legal advice, copyright clearance or trademark clearance;
- guaranteed acceptance by Amazon KDP, Ingram, Apple Books, Kobo, Google Play Books or any other third party;
- paid advertising spend;
- guaranteed sales, rankings, reviews or bestseller status;
- bespoke cover illustration/design beyond features separately included in the customer's software plan or purchased service;
- print manufacturing, proof-copy, shipping or retailer fees;
- translation;
- audiobook production;
- ongoing campaign management after launch-readiness handoff.

## Customer prerequisites

Before fulfillment can complete, the customer must provide/confirm required author/publisher identity, rights declarations, publication metadata, requested formats and any third-party account information that the relevant distribution workflow requires.

ISBN assignment remains validation-gated and is not triggered merely by payment.

## Operational requirements before sale

Before this SKU is enabled for production checkout, ScrollLibrary operations must define:

- responsible human owner/queue;
- service-window duration;
- acknowledgement target;
- expected turnaround target for each review round;
- supported communication channel;
- escalation/refund process when ScrollLibrary cannot deliver the covered service;
- evidence that an order can be tracked from paid -> in progress -> awaiting customer -> completed/refunded.

Until those operational requirements are confirmed, the package should remain marked unavailable after validation rather than accepting payment.
