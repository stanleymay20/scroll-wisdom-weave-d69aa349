# ScrollLibrary content value audit — 2026-10-04

## Scope

Audit of what users are promised, what subscription/publishing contracts actually grant, and whether ScrollLibrary's current quality evidence supports the strongest customer-facing claims.

This audit is repository/product evidence, not proof of production deployment. ScrollLibrary production Supabase is controlled through Lovable; production migration/function state must be re-verified after the next Lovable-controlled deployment.

## Executive verdict

ScrollLibrary's architecture is capable of substantially stronger output governance than a normal prompt-to-book generator, but near-10/10 full-book quality is not yet empirically proven across the paying tier routes.

Commercial pricing may be economically viable, but high-quality marketing claims must remain separate from commercial activation until representative full-book qualification evidence exists.

## Proven strengths

- Server-authoritative plan limits and usage metering.
- Free/paid model differentiation for chapter generation.
- Stronger Pro/Teams model route for Chief Editor audit and publication-quality rewrites.
- Evidence requirements for governed factual categories and dynamically detected material claims.
- Deterministic QA for incomplete generation, AI artifacts, placeholders, rendering defects, citation gaps, factual-risk cues and technical code evidence.
- Whole-book publication pipeline with editorial review, evidence verification, repair, copyediting, technical audit, publishability QA, canonical production-render certification and final server-owned readiness.
- User ownership/commercial-use terms aligned with product entitlements, subject to applicable law and third-party/source rights.
- Publishing-service ISBN assignment remains validation-gated rather than being sold as a raw identifier.

## Principal quality gap

Ordinary Chief Editor certification currently uses lower eligibility thresholds than the empirical near-10 generation-mode qualification doctrine. Passing ordinary editorial eligibility must therefore never be presented as equivalent to ScrollLibrary Press Certified near-10 quality.

The three quality states are defined in `SCROLLLIBRARY_CONTENT_QUALITY_STANDARD.md`:

- Draft
- Verified
- ScrollLibrary Press Certified

## Empirical evidence gap

The existing provider-backed smoke proves real outline/chapter generation and persistence, but not repeated near-10 quality across complete books.

Standard Text remains fail-closed for any near-10/ScrollLibrary Press Certified capability claim until its five-book representative campaign and required independent human reviews pass.

## Plan-value interpretation

### Free

Strong acquisition value. The user gets a meaningful short-book workflow rather than a token demo. Free quality should be described as draft/verification capable unless separately qualified.

### Creator ($19/month)

Strong value for regular creation and exporting. The 250k monthly word pool is the true text-capacity constraint; 10 projects must not be interpreted as 10 fixed-length full books. Creator uses the paid Flash generation route and Flash editorial route.

### Pro ($69/month)

Best individual publishing value. 1M monthly words, longer chapters, KDP export access and a stronger Gemini Pro editorial/rewrite route create real differentiation. Pro should be the primary individual plan positioned for professional publishing once the Pro route passes empirical qualification.

### Teams ($199/month)

Strong pooled economics when collaboration/organization workflows are proven. Do not justify Teams price with dormant PMF-disabled features. Validate five-seat organization behavior before opening Teams checkout.

## Pricing/transparency corrections

1. Present audio credits as narration minutes because the current backend meters approximately one audio credit per standard narration minute.
2. Explain that one visual credit corresponds to one generated visual request under the current contract.
3. Explain that book projects are creation slots and that the monthly AI-word allowance governs generated text capacity.
4. Label marketplace fee discounts as conditional on marketplace GA until seller payment/payout settlement is enabled and proven.
5. Publishing bundles must not cost more than equivalent Single Edition purchases unless they disclose additional included service value.
6. Assisted Publishing Launch requires a concrete service-level scope before sale (human review rounds, metadata QA, format preparation, distribution checklist, revisions, support and exclusions).

## Recommended publishing package ladder

Subject to final margin review:

- Single Edition: $49
- Print + Digital: $89
- Complete Edition: $129
- Assisted Publishing Launch: $399

The first three prices create rational bundle savings while retaining premium pricing for governed publishing services.

## Release decision

Do not delay commercial GA merely because near-10 quality qualification is incomplete, provided pricing/checkout/entitlement/refund/production gates are proven and customer copy is truthful.

However, do not use claims such as “near-10/10,” “professionally publication-ready,” or “ScrollLibrary Press Certified” for a generation route until its empirical qualification evidence passes the controlled doctrine.
