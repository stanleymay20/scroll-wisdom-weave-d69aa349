# Human reviewer independence

For empirical generation-route qualification, a human reviewer should not be the model that generated or audited the manuscript and should not be instructed to preserve a target score.

Reviewers may be editors, subject-matter reviewers or qualified independent readers. Their relationship must be declared in the review record.

The reviewer must read the complete book, record evidence for material weaknesses and answer the publication-usability questions without seeing a desired pass/fail score target during the qualitative assessment where practical.
