# Qualification sample provenance

Every corpus sample must preserve enough provenance to reproduce and interpret its result:

- corpus sample ID;
- generated book ID;
- tier route;
- generation provider/model;
- audit/rewrite provider/model;
- generation prompt/policy version;
- audit prompt version;
- generation timestamp;
- final manuscript scope hash;
- machine gate results;
- human review record;
- attempt/regeneration/repair counts.

Without provenance, a high score is not admissible qualification evidence.
