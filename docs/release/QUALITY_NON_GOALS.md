# Content quality non-goals

The certification system does not promise:

- zero factual error in all circumstances;
- external peer review;
- legal, medical or financial professional advice;
- guaranteed retailer acceptance;
- guaranteed commercial performance;
- that every Draft is ready to publish;
- that a high deterministic QA score alone equals literary excellence.

The goal is auditable, fail-closed quality governance with evidence proportional to the strength of the claim.
