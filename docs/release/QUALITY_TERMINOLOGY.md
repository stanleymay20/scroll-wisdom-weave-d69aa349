# Quality terminology

Use these exact meanings consistently:

- **Draft**: generated/editable; no publication-quality claim.
- **Verified**: passed applicable current-scope integrity/technical/evidence/production gates; not a 9.5/10 literary claim.
- **ScrollLibrary Press Certified**: strict premium manuscript certification under the controlled standard.
- **Generation-route qualified**: empirical evidence shows the tier/model route repeatedly meets the near-10 doctrine on a representative corpus.

A manuscript can be Press Certified only under the strict manuscript gates; a route can be marketed as reliably Certified-capable only after empirical route qualification.
