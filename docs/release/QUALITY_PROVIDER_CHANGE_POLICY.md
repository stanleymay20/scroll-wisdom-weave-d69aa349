# Provider/model change requalification policy

A generation route's empirical qualification is tied to the effective provider/model/prompt/policy combination.

Requalification is required after a material change to:

- generation model/provider;
- Chief Editor/rewrite model/provider;
- master generation prompt or book-type contract;
- evidence-research/verification strategy;
- certification thresholds or scoring rubric;
- production renderer in a way that can materially affect delivered content.

Minor non-content operational changes may use regression evidence rather than a full campaign when they cannot affect manuscript quality; the reason must be documented.
