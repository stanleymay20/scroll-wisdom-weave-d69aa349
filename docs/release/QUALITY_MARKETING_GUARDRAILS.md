# Quality marketing guardrails

Until empirical qualification passes for the exact tier/model route, avoid absolute claims such as:

- "10/10 books every time"
- "publication-ready automatically"
- "factually guaranteed"
- "human-quality guaranteed"
- "peer reviewed"
- "academically certified"

Preferred truthful language:

- "AI-assisted book creation with publication-quality verification gates"
- "Drafts are separated from verified publication candidates"
- "Factual and technical books undergo additional evidence/code checks before certification"
- "ScrollLibrary Press Certified is awarded only when the defined current-scope quality gates pass"

Any numeric quality claim must point to current empirical evidence rather than a rubric threshold alone.
