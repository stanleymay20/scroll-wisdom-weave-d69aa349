# ScrollLibrary / ScrollLibrary Press

## Publishing and knowledge-trust infrastructure

**ScrollLibrary** is building infrastructure for authors, publishers, libraries and institutions to create, validate, publish, distribute and preserve books with a transparent chain of publication identity, rights and evidence.

**ScrollLibrary Press** is the publishing imprint for ScrollLibrary-controlled editions.

## What we provide

### Publication identity
- format- and edition-aware ISBN governance
- immutable publication/edition identity
- cryptographic content hashes
- version, correction, supersession and retraction history
- public verification

### Rights and provenance
- publisher/imprint identity records
- author and rights-holder records
- rights/provenance evidence
- controlled publication snapshots
- audit history

### Validation
- editorial, evidence, QA, structural, production and rights gates
- validation bound to the exact publication state
- support for independent-review separation
- roadmap to public Validation Passports and discipline-specific validation profiles

### Trade readiness
- professional print and digital publication outputs
- metadata quality controls
- format-specific identifiers
- ONIX-oriented trade metadata roadmap
- pricing, territory, availability and distributor readiness

### Library and institutional readiness
- library metadata export roadmap
- accessibility metadata
- controlled institutional collections
- grounded AI discovery across licensed content
- edition/correction visibility
- rights-aware access controls

## Why the model is different

Most publication systems tell a buyer that a book exists.

ScrollLibrary is designed to make it possible to determine:

- who published this edition;
- which exact edition is being read;
- whether the publication has changed since validation;
- who holds relevant rights;
- which validation gates have passed;
- whether a correction, supersession or retraction exists;
- whether the presented file matches the registered publication state.

This does not mean ScrollLibrary declares every proposition in a book to be true. The objective is a transparent evidence and accountability layer that helps readers, booksellers, librarians and institutions evaluate a publication responsibly.
