# ScrollLibrary Wave 1 Pilot Targets

Status: Research-backed target list
Date: 2026-09-12

This is an engagement shortlist, not a claim that any organisation has agreed to partner.

## Tier A — Ghana Library Authority

### Why first

The Ghana Library Authority currently publicly invites authors and publishers to submit works for its national digital library programme and advertises a partnership route. This is directly aligned with ScrollLibrary Press's need to prove institutional acquisition and digital-library acceptance.

### Public acquisition path currently displayed

- acquisitions@library.gov.gh
- Head Office: Ghana Library Authority, Thorpe Road, Accra

Verify immediately before outreach.

### Pilot ask

Do not begin by proposing a nationwide technology replacement.

Ask for a controlled conversation around:

- submitting 1-3 professionally prepared ScrollLibrary Press titles through the existing acquisition/review route;
- understanding the Authority's metadata, rights, DRM, accessibility and review requirements;
- showing a sample Validation Passport as optional supporting evidence;
- exploring whether a later technical pilot around validated digital collections would be useful.

### Success evidence

- titles accepted into review pipeline;
- acquisition metadata accepted/corrected;
- clear institutional feedback on validation fields;
- one title acquired/approved, if merited;
- requirements captured for Library Readiness Gate.

## Tier A — Ghana Publishers Association

### Why

Industry legitimacy, publisher relationships, training, book-fair access and introductions. The Association publicly describes itself as Ghana's collective trade voice for book publishers.

### First ask

- introduction to ScrollLibrary Press;
- membership/participation requirements;
- feedback on Ghana-market publishing/distribution expectations;
- introductions to suitable printers, booksellers and publishers;
- explore a future demonstration session rather than asking for endorsement.

## Tier A — Ingram / IngramSpark

### Why

Global distribution infrastructure connecting publishers/self-publishers with a large network of bookstores, libraries, universities and online retailers.

### Pilot ask

- onboard 3-5 trade-ready titles;
- verify format/metadata/discount/returns configuration;
- execute one real bookstore order and one institutional/library order where feasible;
- compare print/fulfilment economics with regional printing.

## Tier A — VLB / MVB

### Why

VLB is a central bibliographic platform for the German-language book trade. Proper registration makes titles discoverable through normal German trade systems and carries binding price/availability metadata responsibilities.

### Pilot ask

- confirm ScrollLibrary Press publisher/title registration path;
- validate ISBN registrant/main-publisher consistency;
- map ScrollLibrary metadata to VLB/ONIX requirements;
- register the first suitable German-market pilot edition only after trade metadata passes internal readiness checks.

## Tier A — Libri / BoD

### Why

German/DACH print-on-demand and book-trade fulfilment route integrated with Libri's distribution infrastructure.

### Pilot ask

- evaluate ScrollLibrary Press publisher onboarding;
- compare POD, availability, fulfilment, returns and margin model;
- test a single suitable paperback title before broader adoption.

## Tier A — Gardners

### Why

Gardners currently advertises publisher distribution to independent booksellers, chain retailers, schools, public libraries and international customers.

### Public distribution contact currently displayed

- distribution@gardners.com

Verify immediately before outreach.

### Pilot ask

- publisher/distribution eligibility call;
- requirements for stock vs POD-linked supply;
- UK/EU institutional reach;
- terms, returns, payment cycle and metadata feeds;
- fit for a 3-5 title pilot catalogue.

## Tier B — Technische Hochschule Brandenburg Library

### Why

Nearby academic library with electronic media and research-support services. Useful as an early requirements-interview target even if it does not become a commercial pilot.

### First ask

A short librarian/research-services interview around:

- acquisition metadata expected from small/independent publishers;
- e-book licensing;
- MARC/MARCXML and discovery requirements;
- accessibility metadata;
- whether an edition-bound validation passport would be useful or redundant;
- concerns around AI-grounded discovery over licensed collections.

Do not begin by asking the library to buy an unfinished product.

## Tier B — University Library Potsdam

### Why

The library publicly operates formal literature-acquisition processes and research/publication support. It is a useful German academic-library requirements partner.

### First ask

- requirements interview with appropriate subject/acquisition or publication-support staff;
- feedback on metadata package and institutional licensing model;
- potential later controlled collection pilot.

## Tier B — Afram Publications

### Why

Established Ghanaian educational/literary publisher with local subject expertise and school-market experience.

### First ask

Explore one of three paths, without forcing co-publishing:

1. specialist publishing/editorial collaboration;
2. selected co-publication where both sides add measurable value;
3. Afram using ScrollLibrary validation/digital infrastructure for a future controlled catalogue experiment.

## Tier B — Sub-Saharan Publishers

### Why

Ghana-based African publisher with international-facing experience.

### First ask

- understand African/international distribution pain points;
- test whether ScrollLibrary's publication trust/metadata layer solves a real publisher problem;
- explore selected digital, institutional or co-publishing opportunities only after problem fit is established.

## Engagement order

Recommended first sequence:

1. Ghana Library Authority — acquisition requirements and controlled title submission.
2. Ghana Publishers Association — ecosystem introduction.
3. Ingram — global distribution pilot requirements.
4. VLB/MVB — German trade metadata path.
5. Libri/BoD — DACH fulfilment economics.
6. Gardners — UK/EU distribution discussion.
7. TH Brandenburg / University Potsdam — institutional requirements interviews.
8. Afram / Sub-Saharan — publisher-platform and selective co-publishing discovery.

## Rule

No organisation should be described publicly as a ScrollLibrary partner until a written agreement, accepted pilot or other verifiable relationship exists.
